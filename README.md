## 💡 How to Choose an Aircraft Maintenance System

When selecting an aircraft maintenance tracking platform:

1. 🛡️ **Verify Airworthiness Directive Integration**: Ensure the system automates or simplifies tracking of FAA ADs and manufacturer Service Bulletins (SBs).
2. 📱 **Mobile Work Card Support**: Technicians in the hangar benefit immensely from mobile offline access for signing off work orders.
3. 📦 **Inventory & Parts Traceability**: Look for FAA Form 8130-3 or EASA Form 1 digital record attachments for lot tracking.
4. 💸 **Total Cost of Ownership**: Evaluate per-tail vs per-user pricing models based on fleet scale.

## ❓ Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

<details>
<summary><b>1. What is Aircraft Maintenance Management Software (MRO Software)?</b></summary>
<p>Aircraft Maintenance Management Software (MRO/CAMO software) helps airlines, aircraft operators, and repair stations track scheduled maintenance, Airworthiness Directives (ADs), Service Bulletins (SBs), component hours/cycles, inventory, work orders, and technician sign-offs in accordance with aviation safety regulations.</p>
</details>

<details>
<summary><b>2. Can open-source software be used for FAA/EASA certified maintenance?</b></summary>
<p>Yes, provided the software maintains verifiable data integrity, audit logs, and compliant maintenance recordkeeping as mandated under FAA 14 CFR Part 43 / Part 145 or EASA Part-145 regulations. Open-source solutions like MyTailLog or Simple Aircraft Manager offer transparent data ownership ideal for GA and custom workflows.</p>
</details>

<details>
<summary><b>3. What is the difference between CAMO and MRO software?</b></summary>
<p>CAMO (Continuing Airworthiness Management Organisation) software focuses on airworthiness compliance, maintenance planning, and technical records oversight. MRO software focuses on hangar execution, shop floor work orders, inventory management, labor tracking, and billing.</p>
</details>
