# Awesome-Aircraft-Maintenance-Management

## Top Aircraft Maintenance Management Ecosystem



**Curated List of SaaS Products & Open-Source GitHub Projects**  

*Focused on Airworthiness Compliance, MRO Operations & Fleet Maintenance Tracking*  

**Last updated: March 2026**



This repository tracks notable **SaaS platforms** and **open-source projects** for **Aircraft Maintenance Management**. These tools manage airworthiness directives, maintenance scheduling, work orders, component tracking, inventory, and regulatory compliance for airlines, MROs, CAMOs, and aircraft operators.



**Examples** include Ramco Aviation, AMOS by Swiss-AS, Veryon Tracking+, Rusada ENVISION, TRAX, eMRO by Lufthansa Technik, CAMP Systems, IFS Maintenix, Corridor Aviation, and Quantum MX (the category leaders).



**Open-source emphasis**: This section is expanded with active projects for self-hosting, custom maintenance workflows, and transparent airworthiness tracking — ideal for GA owners, small operators, research institutions, and developers building vendor-independent aviation maintenance solutions.



Contributions welcome! Open a PR to add/update entries. Keep descriptions factual and link to official sites.

## SaaS/Hosted Platforms



- **[Ramco Aviation](https://www.ramco.com/aviation/)**  

  Comprehensive aviation maintenance and engineering suite covering MRO, CAMO, and fleet management for airlines and defense operators.



- **[AMOS by Swiss-AS](https://www.swiss-as.com/)**  

  Industry-leading MRO software covering maintenance, engineering, logistics, and finance for airlines and MROs worldwide.



- **[Veryon Tracking+](https://veryon.com/)**  

  Aviation maintenance tracking and compliance platform for business aviation, helicopters, and GA operators with AD/SB tracking.



- **[Rusada ENVISION](https://www.rusada.com/)**  

  Airworthiness, maintenance, and flight operations software for airlines, MROs, and rotary wing operators, with digital task cards and AD/SB management .



- **[TRAX](https://www.trax.aero/)**  

  Cloud-based aviation maintenance and engineering software for airlines, MROs, and suppliers.



- **[eMRO by Lufthansa Technik](https://www.lufthansa-technik.com/emro)**  

  Digital MRO platform from Lufthansa Technik covering maintenance execution, planning, and engineering.



- **[CAMP Systems](https://www.campsystems.com/)**  

  Maintenance tracking and compliance management for business aviation, helicopters, and engines.



- **[IFS Maintenix](https://www.ifs.com/)**  

  Aviation maintenance management software for airlines, MROs, and defense organizations with integrated supply chain.



- **[Corridor Aviation](https://www.corridor.aero/)**  

  Maintenance tracking and compliance software for business aviation operators and maintenance providers.



- **[Quantum MX](https://www.quantummx.com/)**  

  Maintenance management software for GA, flight schools, and small MROs with work order and inventory tracking.



## Open-Source GitHub Projects



- **[CSAT Maintenance Planner](https://github.com/kbss-cvut/csat-maintenance-planner)**  

  Maintenance planner for CSAT (Czech Airlines Technics), developed in cooperation with Czech Technical University. Provides a visual overview of passed, ongoing, and future maintenance plans via a React/TypeScript frontend over REST API. Dockerized for easy deployment .



- **[MyTailLog](https://github.com/iiamit/MyTailLog)**  

  Free, open-source aircraft logbook digitizer and maintenance tracker for GA owners. AI reads paper logbooks using Anthropic vision models; tracks ADs, inspections, weight & balance, and hours. Built on Next.js, Supabase, and Firebase App Hosting with browser-side image processing and zero marginal cost target. MIT licensed .



- **[MSAT (USAF Maintenance Scheduling Application Tool)](https://github.com/Rusty112358/MSAT)**  

  USAF maintenance scheduling tool that imports 200+ text file reports to manage aircraft configuration and maintenance. Provides data mining and reporting for aircraft schedulers and maintenance managers. Designed to support all Wings and any aircraft type. Demonstrates handling of complex legacy data migration .



- **[Atlas CMMS](https://github.com/Grashjs/cmms)**  

  Open-source CMMS with free self-hosted version under GPLv3. Enterprise-grade power with open-source flexibility, covering asset tracking, work orders, preventive maintenance, inventory, and mobile-first technician access. Built on Spring Boot/React. Aviation is one of the listed industries served .



- **[openMAINT](https://www.openmaint.org/)**  

  Open-source facility and asset management built on the CMDBuild framework. Provides space and asset inventory, GIS/BIM support, logistics management, facility management, and economic management. Listed for rail and air transport industries. Supports integration with Alfresco, Liferay, GeoServer, and BIMServer .



- **[OpenAirport](https://github.com/thunderai/openairport)**  

  Open-source airport computer maintenance and management system (CMMS) designed for airports from the ground up. Modular architecture focused on Part 139 compliance (sections 327, 333, 337, 339). Note: development appears stalled (last commits 2012-2015) .



- **[SkyTrack](https://github.com/Anantha1605/SkyTrack)**  

  Aviation database management system tracking aircraft, flight bookings, passenger information, staff assignments, and fleet maintenance records. Goal includes tracking aircraft usage for efficient maintenance scheduling. Potential extension for automated maintenance tracking based on flight hours .



### Additional Strong Open-Source Options



- **SOFINARE Workflow Management** — Research case study implementing a PHP/MySQL web application for EASA Part-145 maintenance organization workflow management, covering work orders, worksheets, and EASA Form 1 issuance .

- **Airmedt** — Research decision support system (DSS) prototype developed with HTML/JavaScript for MRO maintenance decision-making. Focused on repair options analysis, cost estimation, and stakeholder consultation workflows .

- **Planeación Control Producción Aeronáutico (TM Air)** — Academic software project from Universidad Pontificia Bolivariana for aircraft maintenance planning and production control, covering inventory, ADs, service bulletins, and personnel management .



**Frameworks for building custom aircraft maintenance solutions**: For GA owners and small operators, **MyTailLog** provides an AI-powered logbook digitization foundation with minimal hosting costs . For MRO workflow management, **SOFINARE** case study offers a PHP/MySQL blueprint for EASA Part-145 compliance . For scheduling and configuration management, **MSAT** demonstrates handling complex legacy data with data mining techniques . **Atlas CMMS** and **openMAINT** provide general-purpose CMMS foundations adaptable to aviation needs .
